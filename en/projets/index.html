Fikajiana

Write maths by hand, the app does the sums.

Write 2+3= and the answer appears in handwriting after the equals sign. Equations, fractions, derivatives, integrals, matrices, graphs and 40 geometry tools, on a sheet that fills the screen.

  • Android
  • Offline
  • Open source

L Katta

A little lemur assistant on your Linux desktop.

A Malagasy lemur lives on your screen: it finds your files, reminds you of appointments and answers questions with a free AI, no account or key needed. Six species to choose from.

  • Linux
  • Free
  • Open source

matOS

Mathematics on your computer, without internet.

A Linux distribution on an 8 GB USB stick: SageMath, GeoGebra, R, LaTeX and 23 free first- and second-year textbooks, already installed. Lean, Coq and offline Wikipedia come as add-on modules. It grew out of the Math x Mad club.

  • Linux
  • USB stick
  • Offline
  • Open source
Discover matOS
